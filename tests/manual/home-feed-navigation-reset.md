# Home Feed Navigation Reset

## YouTube logo refresh

1. Enable the Main page filter and open the YouTube home page.
2. Wait until at least one old recommendation is marked as not interested.
3. Click the YouTube logo without reloading the browser tab.
4. Confirm old recommendations in the refreshed feed are detected and processed.
5. Confirm processed recommendations show `[N일전 업로드]` and the video title in YouTube's hidden-video result card.
6. Confirm the browser console has no extension errors.
