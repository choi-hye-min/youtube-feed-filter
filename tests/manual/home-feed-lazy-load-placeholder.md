# Home Feed Lazy-load Placeholder

1. Enable the Main page filter and open the YouTube home page.
2. Wait until at least one old recommendation becomes a placeholder.
3. Scroll to the bottom until YouTube appends another batch of recommendations.
4. Confirm existing placeholders remain in their original grid positions.
5. Confirm YouTube's "Video hidden" undo card is not visible beside each placeholder.
6. Repeat the bottom scroll once more and confirm the placeholders still remain.
7. Confirm newly loaded old recommendations also become placeholders.
8. Confirm the feed grid keeps its column alignment and the browser console has no extension errors.
