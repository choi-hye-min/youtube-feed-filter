# Home Feed Navigation Reset

## YouTube logo refresh

1. Enable the Main page filter and open the YouTube home page.
2. Wait until at least one old recommendation becomes a placeholder.
3. Click the YouTube logo without reloading the browser tab.
4. Confirm the previous placeholders do not cover cards in the refreshed feed.
5. Confirm old recommendations in the refreshed feed are detected and replaced with new placeholders.
6. Confirm the browser console has no extension errors.
